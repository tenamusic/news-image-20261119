Entry for make.fun's [news-image-20261119 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261119) — "Sum up 19 November's world news in one image".

`main.py` copies the bundled `entry.png` — a 1024×1024 front-page infographic
summing up Thursday 19 November 2026 in six stories (the first-ever
Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral summit in Shenzhen, Iran saying three oil tankers
hit mines and caught fire east of the Strait of Hormuz, Democrats nominating
Hakeem Jeffries 251–11 as the next House Speaker, the World Court ruling on
Myanmar's alleged Rohingya genocide, GTA VI launching worldwide to a 99 on
Metacritic, and Cyclone Akvan bearing down on India's east coast) — to the
output directory.

The image was drawn with a fresh Python/PIL script (`compose19.py` in the
parent directory) from the day's reporting.
